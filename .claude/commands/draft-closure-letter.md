---
description: Draft the letter to a client when their file closes - what they worked on, what they achieved, how to come back. Drafts to drafts/; a person sends.
---

1. Run `node scripts/cases.mjs client "<client>" --json`.
2. Write `drafts/closure-<file>.md`: warm, short, in plain words. Their goals in their own words and how far they got, the date the file closed, how to come back (the intake number), and where to go after hours.
3. If the client has a safety alert about contact (no voicemail, text only, do not post), say so at the top of the draft and suggest the safe way to deliver it.
4. Nothing about other people in the family that the client did not raise. A person reads and sends.
